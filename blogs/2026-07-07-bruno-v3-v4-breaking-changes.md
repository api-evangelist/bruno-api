Bruno v4 brings meaningful improvements to how secrets are stored, how environment variables behave, and what the collection schema supports. Most of the changes are automatic when you upgrade through the app. But a handful of them require attention before you upgrade, especially if your scripts touch sensitive values or your team runs mixed Bruno versions.

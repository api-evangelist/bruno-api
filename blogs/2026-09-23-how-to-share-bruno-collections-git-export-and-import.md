Applies to Bruno 3.0 and later At some point, there's usually a need to share your API collection. A teammate needs the requests/scripts you built, QA wants your test suite, or a partner wants to try your API without digging through reference docs. How you hand that work over decides whether everyone stays on the same version or ends up with three different copies.
